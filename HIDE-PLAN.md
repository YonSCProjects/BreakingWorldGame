# Atom-card hide plan — The Lattice (68-card kit)

**Why these spots:** they match the in-app **clues** ראש-המסדר gives, so the clues
actually guide the kids (white = low by your feet, red = somewhere warm, grey =
heavy & low, blue = in plain sight, violet/green = corners).

## Rules for whoever hides them (read first)
- **Tape each card in place**, QR facing out, so it stays put — all 3 teams scan
  the *same* cards where they sit (don't let kids pocket them).
- **Kid height or lower. Nothing high, nothing dangerous** — no climbing, not on
  hot radiators or near electrics. "Warm" = sunny sills / beside (not on) a
  heater / the warm back of a PC.
- **Spread across the whole area** — the corridor AND every room — so kids move.
- Keep a few **spare cards** + remember the **`?staff` injector** to rescue a card
  that won't scan or goes missing.
- Place by **colour**, not by number — any white card works for "find 2 white".

## Where each atom hides

| Atom | Colour | # | Theme (matches the clue) | Example school spots |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **מימן** H | white | **28** | tiny & everywhere, **low, by your feet** | chair legs & under seats, table undersides & legs, along the baseboard/skirting, floor corners, bottom shelf of a bookcase, under a doormat edge — scatter widely (it's the most common) |
| **חמצן** O | red | **14** | **warm / glowing** | sunny windowsills, beside a radiator (on the wall, not on it), the warm back of a computer/monitor, near a desk lamp, on a projector's side, by the water-urn |
| **פחמן** C | grey | **10** | **heavy & low, not far** | under the heaviest table, bottom of a filing cabinet/cupboard, under a bench, low on the teacher's-desk leg, bottom shelf of a bookcase, under a PC tower on the floor |
| **חנקן** N | blue | **4** | **calm, in plain sight** | pinned openly on a news board, on the front of a painting frame at eye level, flat on an open desk in the middle, on the whiteboard ledge |
| **נתרן** Na | violet | **3** | **rare & wild, corners** | top corner behind a painting, behind a door / far room corner, the odd high-ish shelf corner |
| **כלור** Cl | green | **3** | **corners** | corner of a news board, behind a plant/object in a room corner, the low spot where two walls meet |
| **TRAPS** He·Ne·Ar | gold | **2+2+2** | shiny "treasure" **decoys** (dead-ends) | the *centre* of a framed painting, a trophy/display cabinet, a shiny computer screen, a headline on the news board, a "prize" spot on the teacher's desk, an obvious lure by the entrance |

**Total = 68 cards** (28 H · 14 O · 10 C · 4 N · 3 Na · 3 Cl · 2 He · 2 Ne · 2 Ar).

## Notes
- The **gold cards are traps** — when scanned the app says *"this one likes to be
  alone — leave it and grab another."* Put them in the most *tempting* spots so a
  kid wastes a happy scan on the dead-end (it's fun, not punishing). Maybe mark
  their backs so staff know which 6 are decoys.
- **Glucose (the bonus)** needs a LOT — 6 grey, 12 white, 6 red — so make sure the
  grey (C) and red (O) spots are genuinely spread out, or the fast teams will
  vacuum one corner. The 28 whites cover it.
- If you want it **harder for older/faster kids**: push a few reds/greys to the
  trickier warm/heavy spots. **Easier:** keep more of them low and obvious.
