# Hour 2 design brief — prompt for an LLM

Paste everything below into an LLM (ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini) to have it design
Hour 2 of the game. It already contains the full Hour-1 context, the students'
profile, and the requirements.

---

You are an expert designer of immersive, physically-active educational games for
children. Help me design HOUR 2 of a two-hour live-action adventure game.

**THE PLAYERS (this drives every decision):**
12–14-year-olds (7th–8th grade) with emotional and behavioural difficulties,
anxiety, ADHD, and often ODD. They have short attention spans and disengage
instantly from anything that feels like school — sitting still, reading,
worksheets, or being told they're "wrong." They thrive on movement, clear simple
rules, frequent small wins, feeling like heroes, having agency (choosing, not
being commanded), teamwork, and constant encouragement. **Hour 2 must feel like
an exciting GAME, never a lesson.** Keep every instruction tiny and simple,
celebrate constantly, and make sure there are no "losers."

**THE WORLD (already established in Hour 1):**
The kids are young KNIGHTS of an ancient order, summoned by **"ראש המסדר"** — the
Head of the Order, a warm, wise, Gandalf-like mentor who appears on a big screen.
He told them they are "chosen ones," each with a unique gift, and that together
they will *bring the world back to life.* They were sorted into three Orders
(teams of 4–6): the **Eagle** (guardians of the **Air**), the **Dolphin**
(guardians of the **Water**), and the **Lion** (guardians of the **Sun**). Each
kid has a team badge and a wand. The setting is a school floor: a wide central
corridor with several rooms branching off it. Each team has a "Seer" (1–2 kids)
at a control laptop in the corridor; the rest are "field" knights who explore. A
phone app and the Seer's screen track progress, give hints in ראש המסדר's voice,
and confirm successes.

**WHAT HAPPENED IN HOUR 1 (in detail):**
The world's building blocks had scattered. Teams hunted hidden, colour-coded
cards representing ATOMS (white = hydrogen, red = oxygen, grey = carbon, etc.)
hidden around the rooms. Using the app, they completed a series of quick
"missions" — each asked them to gather the right atoms, described by **COLOUR,
never chemistry** ("find two white and one red"), to build a MOLECULE. Across the
hour they built several molecules, **including WATER, OXYGEN, and CARBON
DIOXIDE.** When a team gathered the right atoms, they ran back to their Seer; a
costumed staffer checked it and "stabilized" it, and they advanced. It was an
energetic, friendly race between the three Orders — lots of running, hunting,
scanning, and small victories inside the heroic story.

**YOUR TASK — design HOUR 2 (about 60 minutes):**
Now that the kids have built **oxygen, water, and carbon dioxide**, Hour 2 should
teach — through a fun, mostly-physical game — **how these three molecules keep
life on Earth alive, through cellular RESPIRATION and PHOTOSYNTHESIS,** and how
living things depend on one another.

The simple science it must land (without ever sounding like a textbook):
- **Cellular respiration:** every creature's cells burn FOOD (sugar) + OXYGEN to
  make the ENERGY to move and live, and release CARBON DIOXIDE + WATER.
- **Photosynthesis:** plants take CARBON DIOXIDE + WATER + SUNLIGHT and make new
  FOOD + OXYGEN.
- They are mirror images forming a never-ending cycle; the sun powers it all;
  plants and animals keep each other alive.
- A beautiful tie-in: the three things a plant needs are **Air, Water, and Sun**
  — exactly the three Orders (Eagle, Dolphin, Lion) — so the cycle only works
  when all three teams **cooperate.**

**REQUIREMENTS:**
- Lasts ~60 minutes with a clear arc building to an exciting finale.
- **Mostly PHYSICAL** — real-world movement, running, carrying, doing — in the
  corridor and rooms. The app is only a light aid (narration + a progress meter
  on the Seer's screen).
- Fill the hour with **VARIETY** — several short, *different* mini-games, not one
  thing repeated — to hold short attention spans.
- Simple rules, frequent wins, constant encouragement, **no losers.** Turn Hour
  1's friendly race into Hour 2's **cooperation** — the three Orders must unite.
- Stay inside the "knights restoring life to the world" story, with ראש המסדר
  narrating key moments.
- Use simple, cheap props.

**OUTPUT — a practical plan staff can run:**
1. A timed run-sheet (segment by segment, with minutes).
2. For each segment: what the kids physically do, the one simple rule, the props,
   and the single idea it teaches.
3. The exact short lines ראש המסדר says at the key moments (warm, simple,
   encouraging).
4. A full props-to-prepare list.
5. The celebratory finale.

Keep every instruction short and simple — the kids *and the staff* need clarity,
not complexity.
